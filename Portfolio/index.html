A little about me

My name is Christian Angelo C. Refalbor, born and raised in the United Arab Emirates, where I spent 15 years watching skyscrapers rise out of the desert before eventually calling it home. I came back to Davao in 2018, trading one kind of heat for another, and I'm currently an IT student who never really believed in forcing things — I've learned that the best solutions, whether in code or in life, tend to show up when you stop wrestling with them. Some semesters I'm deep in code, untangling logic until 2 AM; other nights I'm chasing a melody on guitar, letting my hands do the thinking my brain is too tired for. Somewhere between the syntax errors and the missed chords, I've found that both pursuits ask the same thing of me: patience, curiosity, and the willingness to start over when something doesn't sound right.

Music taught me to listen before I react. IT taught me to debug before I panic.
